Location: “Since \(7\) is prime and \(7\mid n^2 = n\cdot n\), Euclid's lemma … implies \(7\mid n\)”  
Description: Critical Error: The proof of the implication never explicitly assumes the antecedent \(7\mid n^2\). As written, it asserts \(7\mid n^2\) for an arbitrary integer \(n\), which is false in general. Therefore the application of Euclid’s lemma is unsupported, invalidating the proof of the lemma.
